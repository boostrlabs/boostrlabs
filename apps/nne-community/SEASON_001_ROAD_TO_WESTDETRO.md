# NNE Community — Season 001

## ROAD TO WESTDETRO

Launch window: now through **2026-08-28**.

Core message: **Participar suma. Esforzarte multiplica.**

## Economy

NNE Credits are internal participation/reward points. They are not cash, crypto, or publicly redeemable at a fixed USD rate.

Internally, all awarded credits must be attributable to one of three buckets:

- **Base Credits** — completing the defined quest.
- **Merit Credits** — creativity, effort, quality, consistency.
- **Impact Credits** — measurable performance after publication.

The user sees one NNE Credits balance. The ledger keeps the origin of every credit.

## Repeatable quest rules

### Support Bundle

A single like, single comment, or single repost does **not** earn credits.

A qualifying support bundle is the defined group of actions on the same assigned post, such as:

- like
- comment
- repost/share

Suggested base reward: **75–100 Credits**.

### Comment Run

A user may complete a defined comment-run quest on an assigned post.

- 10 comments on the same assigned post: **250 Credits base**.
- Simple comments and emojis can qualify when the quest is explicitly a volume/support quest.
- The quest must define a completion cap per post.

### Creator Quest

Users may publish multiple original TikToks/Reels around the same song.

Season 001 default:

- Maximum: **10 creator submissions per user per song**.
- Submissions #1–3: **500 Base Credits each**.
- Submissions #4–6: **400 Base Credits each**.
- Submissions #7–10: **300 Base Credits each**.
- Approximate Base Credits cap per song: **3,900 Credits**.

Creativity and performance are reviewed separately from the base cap.

### Merit / Creativity

Artist staff reviews qualifying creator submissions with a controlled rubric:

- Completed
- Good
- Standout
- Exceptional

The reviewer does not type an arbitrary credit amount. The system maps the selected tier to the configured bonus.

Season 001 campaign target: up to roughly **10,000 Merit Credits** per campaign unless NNE Admin overrides it.

### Impact / Performance

Content can be re-reviewed after publication.

Users may request **Claim Performance Bonus** on an already approved submission when they can demonstrate meaningful growth.

Performance rubric:

- Normal
- Strong
- Breakout
- Viral

Impact Credits live outside the normal repeatable/base grind cap. Exceptional performance may receive a large manual NNE Admin bonus with an audit reason.

## Weekly economy guardrail

Season 001 target for normal repeatable Base/Merit activity:

- approximately **10,000–15,000 Credits per user per week** before exceptional Impact bonuses.

This is a calibration guardrail, not a public cash-equivalent promise.

- **NNE Admin** — global visibility, economy configuration, quest/reward administration, overrides and audit.

- **Artist Staff: Gemese** — reviews Gemese-assigned submissions only.
- **Artist Staff: Xiam** — reviews Xiam-assigned submissions only.
- **Member** — completes quests and submits evidence.

Artist staff can:

- approve
- reject
- select creativity tier
- select performance tier
- mark content Featured

Artist staff cannot arbitrarily type credit awards outside configured mappings.

Every award must retain:

- submission
- quest
- artist/content owner
- reviewer
- reward bucket (base / merit / impact)
- amount
- timestamp
- review reason/tier

## Launch principle

Quantity has a cap. **Impact does not have to.**

NNE should always make it possible to grind small support quests, while making original creativity and demonstrated impact the fastest path toward meaningful rewards.
