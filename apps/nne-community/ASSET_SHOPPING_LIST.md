# NNE Community — Premium asset shopping list

Use this list to source or commission the launch visuals. Only provide images NNE has permission to publish. Keep the original master files outside the repository; the web exports listed below are the delivery files.

## Delivery standard

- Product cutouts: transparent PNG master, minimum 1600 × 1600 px; web export in WebP, 1024 × 1024 px, under 250 KB when possible.
- Cover art and square icons: 1400 × 1400 px master; web export in WebP, 768 × 768 px.
- Portraits: minimum 2000 px on the long edge; web export in WebP, 960 × 1200 px.
- Hero art: desktop 2400 × 1350 px and mobile-safe 1080 × 1350 px; WebP under 500 KB.
- Use sRGB, clean edges and no baked-in UI text unless the art itself requires it.
- Keep the subject inside the central 70% safe area so responsive crops do not cut it off.
